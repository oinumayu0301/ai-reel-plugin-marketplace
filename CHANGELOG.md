# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Added `$learn-ai-reel-market` for multi-account market ingestion.
- Added workspace-local persistent intelligence for hooks, scripts, visuals, audio, captions, and CTAs.
- Added account-level influence caps, time decay, duplicate protection, small-sample shrinkage, evidence grades, and promotion thresholds.
- Split learning into exact-account reproduction models and cross-account transferable priors.
- A single account can now update its own model immediately; reuse for another account requires evidence from at least three independent accounts.
- Updated `$reconstruct-ai-reel-spec` to read and refresh the adaptive corpus.

- Initial public URL-to-reproduction-spec workflow.
