# Six-lane creative taxonomy

Encode mechanisms with short lower-case kebab-case pattern names. A pattern must be reusable without copying a creator's identity, exact wording, footage, character, joke, voice, music, or shot order.

## Hook

Capture:

- entry family: `consequence-first`, `recognition-question`, `social-friction`, `visible-anomaly`, `number-led`, `confession`, `viewer-challenge`, or a justified new label;
- first visible event and whether it is understandable within one second;
- audience self-recognition, promise specificity, concrete stakes, curiosity gap, novelty, and first payoff time;
- spoken hook and visual hook alignment;
- likely scroll reason and rival explanation.

Do not equate loudness, shock, or large text with hook quality. Code what question or tension the viewer needs resolved.

## Script

Capture premise complexity, prior knowledge, beat count, progression, information gap, escalation, evidence, re-hook, reversal, payoff, loop, CTA bridge, sentence length, lexical difficulty, audience-language naturalness, spoken-caption information split, likely abandonment point, and rival explanations.

Useful labels include `three-step-escalation`, `partial-answer-early`, `midpoint-reframe`, `meaning-change-ending`, `clean-loop`, and `single-idea-script`.

## Visual

Capture realism, familiar location, recognizable object, human presence, character consistency, action continuity, physical plausibility, first-frame subject scale, framing, camera and actor motion, lighting, palette, depth, visual density, meaningful-change interval, cut interval, generated-media defects, static-image risk, and comprehension cost.

Useful labels include `familiar-real-location`, `immediate-human-action`, `object-led-proof`, `continuous-character`, `motion-every-beat`, and `static-opening-risk`.

## Audio

Capture the audience-appropriate voice profile using broad non-identifying descriptors, language naturalness, speech rate, pitch range, pauses, emphasis, emotional trajectory, pronunciation, music function, song-led versus voice-led delivery, lyric-hook function without retaining lyrics, SFX, re-hook sound change, ambience, silence, loudness balance, and speech masking.

Never encode a real person's identity, voiceprint, cloning reference, lyrics, melody, or imitation instruction. Useful labels include `natural-japanese-young-adult`, `conversational-pacing`, `pause-before-reveal`, `music-led-lyric-hook`, `music-assisted-emotional-lift`, `voice-led-clarity`, and `subtle-rehook-sfx`.

## Caption

Capture relationship to speech, characters per screen, line count, semantic units, exposure duration, synchronization, reading speed, hierarchy, font class, relative size, weight, position, safe-zone compliance, contrast, outline, color, background treatment, silent comprehension, truncation, overlap, glyph failure, and text-generation artifacts.

Useful labels include `summary-not-transcript`, `one-semantic-unit`, `two-line-maximum`, `high-contrast-center-lower`, and `silent-readable`.

## CTA

Capture the primary reaction, prompt family, timing, narrative relevance, cognitive friction, emotional safety, specificity, profile handoff, likely compliance reason, and likely resistance reason.

Useful labels include `binary-choice-comment`, `experience-report-comment`, `save-for-later`, `send-to-specific-person`, `next-episode-follow`, and `generic-engagement-begging`.

## Cross-lane interactions

Record important pairs, such as a consequence-first hook with a summary caption, or a familiar-real-location visual with a conversational voice. Interactions require more independent-account support because combinations become sparse quickly.
