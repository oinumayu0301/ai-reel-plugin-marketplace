# Quality gates

Do not deliver the specification until every applicable gate passes.

## Evidence integrity

- Target URL, collection time, sample size, and stop condition are stated.
- Missing metrics remain blank rather than zero or invented.
- Observations, calculations, inferences, assumptions, and recommendations are distinguishable.
- Evidence grade matches actual coverage.
- Winner/loser claims use matched cohorts or disclose the mismatch.
- Alternative explanations are included for causal claims.
- Any account-specific model matches the exact target URL or platform/handle.
- Every prior applied from other accounts has `transferableAcrossAccounts: true` and sufficient independent-account support.
- No single-account hypothesis is transferred to another target or promoted as a plugin-wide rule.

## Strategic quality

- Market-in demand and product-out differentiation are separately defined.
- The selected position is at their intersection.
- Ten concepts are materially different and span multiple pillars and emotional engines.
- Each concept has one primary objective, no more than one secondary objective, and one decision-linked CTA.
- The selected concept includes a likely failure and a test variable.

## Originality and safety

- No real creator impersonation or voice imitation.
- No copied character, footage, script, joke, wording, music, or shot sequence.
- Transferable mechanisms are separated from expression and asset IP.
- New premise, examples, world, wording, payoff, voice, visual treatment, and shot order are specified.

## Production readiness

- The hook is visually understandable within one second.
- Spoken lines are natural for the target audience.
- On-screen text summarizes rather than duplicates speech.
- Text fits safe zones, uses short semantic units, and is readable on a phone.
- The video remains broadly understandable with sound off.
- Every shot has purpose, duration, continuity, and rejection criteria.
- Realistic motion is specified for every intended moving shot; no accidental static-image opening.
- Character, location, prop, wardrobe, lighting, framing, audio, and export rules are explicit.

## Forecast honesty

- Predictions are low/base/high ranges with an observation window.
- Baseline cohort and normalization method are named.
- Likes and comments are tied to visible rates only when inputs exist.
- Follows are conditional unless profile-visit and conversion data exist.
- Positive drivers, downside drivers, confidence, and invalidation signals are stated.
- No guarantee language or unsupported precision remains.
