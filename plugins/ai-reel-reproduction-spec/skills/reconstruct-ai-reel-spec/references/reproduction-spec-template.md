# Reproduction specification template

Use this order. Keep evidence, calculations, inferences, assumptions, and generated recommendations visibly distinct.

## 0. Status strip

- Target URL / platform / analyzed at
- Evidence grade and confidence
- Accessible posts / playable videos / visible-metric posts
- Primary objective / secondary objective
- Key assumptions and collection stop condition

## 1. Executive verdict

- What the account appears to win on
- What is transferable
- What must not be copied
- Biggest evidence limitation
- Recommended original position in one sentence

## 2. Evidence manifest

Source-level inventory and missing fields. Include links or stable identifiers when available.

## 3. Account mechanism fingerprint

Audience promise, topic engine, emotional engines, hook families, progression, payoff, loop, visual grammar, audio grammar, CTA, series system, profile conversion path, production system, and rights boundary.

## 4. Winner/loser comparisons

Matched-pair table with alternative explanations and confidence. Add micro-timeline analysis for representative playable posts only.

## 5. Market-in map

Audience, common situation, pain/desire, desired feeling, viewing context, and reason to share/save/comment/follow.

## 6. Product-out map

Owned expertise, original character/world, production capability, proof, visual/audio assets, sustainability constraints, and series advantage.

## 7. Strategic intersections

Three market-in/product-out intersections and the selected account promise.

## 8. Transfer map

| Observed element | Mechanism | Replaceable expression | Protected/identity element | Original application | Evidence strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 9. Original portfolio — 10 concepts

For each: title, one-line premise, pillar, emotional engine, primary/secondary objective, hook, three-step progression, payoff/loop, CTA, score vector, likely failure, test variable, and production difficulty.

## 10. Selected flagship

Selection rationale, rejected alternatives, audience promise, objective, novelty, series promise, and expected failure mode.

## 11. Full script and beat sheet

| Time | Spoken line | On-screen summary | Visual action | Camera/edit | Audio | Beat purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

On-screen text summarizes speech and must work with sound off. Include a re-hook or reveal around 55–75% and a resolved or looping ending.

## 12. Production bible

Original character, realistic world/location, prop/wardrobe, lighting, lens/camera behavior, framing, motion, palette, typography, text size and safe zones, edit rhythm, audio/voice, continuity rules, negative constraints, and rights-safety rules.

## 13. Generation and assembly pack

Shot prompts, negative prompts, reference requirements, duration per shot, transition notes, voice specification without impersonation, caption plan, sound mix, cover, post caption, pinned comment, and export settings.

## 14. Reaction forecast

Low/base/high views, likes, comments, and conditional follows. Include window, basis, confidence, positive drivers, downside drivers, and invalidation signals.

## 15. Validation plan

Ordered tests, one dominant variable per test, sample/window, threshold, decision rule, and learning value. Include a pre-publish quality checklist and post-publish data request.

## 16. Missing evidence request

Prioritize only the inputs that would materially change the decision: representative downloads, post dates, visible metrics, Insights exports, profile visits, follows, retention, shares, or saves.
